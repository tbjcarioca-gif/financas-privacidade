Privacy Policy — Cabe no bolso?

Kodrix Labs · effective November 15, 2026 · Português

Summary: your financial data stays on your phone only. We have no server and never receive your entries, bills, cards or amounts.

1. What stays on your phone

  • Entries, recurring bills, cards, goals and savings boxes you create.
  • This data is stored only on the device. It is not sent to Kodrix Labs or anyone else.
  • The app does not ask for your name, e-mail, tax ID or bank passwords.

2. What the app fetches from the internet

  • Exchange rates: from the Central Bank of Brazil (PTAX) and open.er-api.com. The app only requests rates; it sends none of your data.
  • Interest rates (Selic, CDI, TR): from the Central Bank of Brazil, for the savings simulation. The app only requests rates; it sends none of your data.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

  • The free version shows a Google AdMob banner.
  • Ads are always non-personalized: they do not use your profile or interests.
  • AdMob still receives the device advertising ID, IP address and technical device data to serve ads, count views and prevent fraud. See Google's policy.
  • You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings (Google → Ads).
  • Where the law requires it (for example, in the European Union), the app asks for your consent before showing ads.

4. Purchase to remove ads (Google Play)

  • Payment is handled by Google Play. Kodrix Labs never receives your card or payment details.
  • The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase exists, to remove the ads.

5. Voice

  • If you use voice entry, the audio is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (usually Google's). The app only receives the text.
  • Microphone permission is only requested when you tap the voice button.

6. App password

  • If you turn on the app password, it stays only on your phone, stored in a way that not even the app can read it back. It is not included in backups and never leaves the device.
  • Fingerprint, face and the phone's screen lock are checked by Android itself. The app only receives "unlocked" or "not unlocked".

7. Your rights

  • Since financial data stays on your phone, you control it: you can export it as a spreadsheet (CSV) from the app.
  • To delete everything: use Settings → Delete data in the app, uninstall the app, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Cabe no bolso? → Storage → Clear data.
  • For data handled by Google (ads and purchase), use your Google account tools or contact Google.

8. Children

The app is not designed for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
